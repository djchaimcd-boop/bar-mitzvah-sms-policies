Terms & Conditions

The rules for the Bar Mitzvah Guest List SMS program, a private text-message tool for compiling one family's guest list.

What this program is

This is a personal, non-commercial SMS program operated by Chaim Leshinsky (sole proprietor) to help one family member record guest names and addresses for a bar mitzvah by text message. It is not sold, marketed, or made available to the public.

How to join

You opt in by texting a message to the program's dedicated number. Because you initiate contact yourself, sending the first message is your consent to receive replies from this program. No one is added without first texting in.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is driven entirely by your own activity: you get one reply for each message you send, plus occasional status updates on request. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Keywords

STOP
Cancels your participation immediately. You will receive no further messages.
HELP
Returns support instructions and a way to reach the operator directly.

Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission from your network operator.

Changes to these terms

Because this program serves a single event with a fixed end date, these terms will not be expanded or repurposed for another use without notifying participants first.
