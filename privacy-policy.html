Bar Mitzvah Guest List SMS Program Effective July 30, 2026

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Bar Mitzvah Guest List SMS program collects, how it is used, and how your mobile number is protected.

Who runs this program

This SMS program is operated by Chaim Leshinsky, a sole proprietor, for a single private purpose: helping one family member compile a guest list for a bar mitzvah by text message. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

Information we collect

When you text the program's number, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • The content of the messages you send (typically names and mailing addresses you choose to share)
  • The date and time of each message, for basic recordkeeping

How we use it

Information you send is used solely to build a private guest list for one event and to reply to you with confirmations, such as acknowledging a name was added or an address was recorded. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond this single event.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number. Your phone number and message content are never shared with third parties, affiliates, or other users of this program, and are never used for marketing by us or anyone else.

The only exception is our SMS delivery provider (Twilio), which transmits messages on our behalf under its own confidentiality obligations, and is never permitted to use your number for its own marketing.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your own texting activity — you receive one reply for each message you send, plus occasional status updates you request. There is no recurring or scheduled messaging. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

How long we keep it

Guest list information is retained only until the event has taken place, after which it is deleted. You may request earlier deletion or a copy of your data at any time using the contact details below.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages and be removed from the program. Reply HELP for support, or contact us directly using the details below.
